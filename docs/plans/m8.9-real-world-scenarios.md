# M8.9 Real-world scenarios

Status: approved; M8.9a, M8.9b, M8.9c, M8.9d, M8.9e, M8.9f, M8.9g, M8.9h and M8.9i done; the plan stays until the user has reviewed the run and says "finish". Runs before M8.1 (`docs/plans/m8.1-materials.md`), whose real-world criteria point to these scenarios.

## Goal

Real experiments the game should reproduce (ice melting in a cup of water, steam scalding a cold box, a red-hot bar cooling, sand running through an hourglass, lava spreading and growing a crust) become tests whose targets come from real data, not from the game's own output, so every later change shows whether the game got more or less realistic. Those the game cannot pass yet are marked as waiting for the work that will make them pass, and the rest of the catalogue is listed as to-do tests.

## Scope

In: the scenario test helper; a module of scenario scenes, each opened by `?scene=<name>`; nine new scenarios and five existing tests renamed as scenarios, each with a control; every other scenario of `docs/research/real-world-scenarios.md` as a `test.todo`; the research doc's Rules in `AGENTS.md`; M8.9 in the roadmap; backlog lines for the mechanics the waiting scenarios need; M8.1's plan pointed to its scenarios, with ALUMINIUM; the out-of-reach scenarios named where they are ruled out; the research doc deleted.

Not in M8.9: any change to a mechanic, constant or element row (a scenario that misses its target waits; it is not fixed here), new elements, CI (the GPU suites still skip there, backlog D11).

## Terms

- **Scenario**: as the research doc's "What a scenario is". Its name is its scene's and starts its test titles; a version goes after it, `iceCup (full): ...`.
- **Waiting**: `it.fails` titled `<name> [waits for <IDs>]: <outcome>`. Its **companion** is a normal test, `<name>: measures <quantity> in <range>`, whose range holds both today's value and the target, so only a crash or a meaningless value fails it.
- **Control**: the same scene on a test table (`Simulation`'s `elements` option) with one property deliberately wrong; titled `<name> control: <what is wrong> misses <target>`, it passes when the value lies outside the target.

## Design

- **No mechanic changes.** The run edits `src/` only in `src/scenarios.ts`, `src/scene-canvas.ts` and `src/scenes.ts`'s imports and registry. A scenario that misses is never fixed here, and its target never moves toward the game's value.
- **Scenes.** The runtime scene primitives (`Canvas`, `packCell`, `startTemp`, `tank`, `WALL`) move from `src/scenes.ts` to `src/scene-canvas.ts`, importers updated, so `src/scenes.ts` can spread `SCENARIO_SCENES` from `src/scenarios.ts` into `SCENES` without an import cycle; the `Scene` and `Chamber` types stay (a type-only import is no runtime cycle). A scenario with variants (two layers, two outlets, fast and full) registers its main one, and its tests call the builder with the others. The five renamed scenarios keep their setups in their test files: moving them would change no measurement, and two cannot be a `?scene=` (`freezingPond`'s held ceiling is a test row, `sandPile` is poured by the brush).
- **Helper** (`tests/scenario.ts`). `scenario({ name, version?, measure, checks, timeout })`: `measure` runs the scene once (memoized promise) and returns named values; each check gives its value's name, outcome text, target range, `waitsFor` IDs (if any) and the companion's range. `control({ name, wrong, measure, target })`. Both are called inside the file's `describe.skipIf(!device)`.
- **Files.** `tests/scenarios-heat.test.ts` (`iceCup`, `steamBurn`, `contactTemp`, `redHotBar`, `lavaCrust`, `heatedLayer`), `tests/scenarios-flow.test.ts` (`hourglass`, `viscousSpread`, `freeFall`), `tests/scenarios-todo.test.ts` (the catalogue, grouped by what each needs). An item that builds a todo moves it into a test file of its topic (M8.1: `tests/materials.test.ts`).
- **Seeds.** When built, each scenario is measured on three seeds and the spread logged under its item; its test runs one seed, or the mean of the three when the spread is more than a quarter of the tolerance's width.
- **Expected pass that misses** (AC4 to AC6, AC10, the renamed five): check the test by hand first (the calculation, the control, the measure). If it still misses, it waits on a new backlog line (B when the cause is a bug, I when a mechanic is missing, "cause not found" otherwise) that gives the measured value, and the final report lists it for the user.
- **Suite time.** M8.9b records `npm test`'s duration. Measured today: 60,000 supersteps of a small sealed scene take 3 s, 30,000 with air (4 supersteps per call) 6 to 7 s. If the scenarios add more than 3 minutes, M8.9i moves the full versions, and any scenario test over 60 s, to `npm run test:full` (`vitest.full.config.ts` over `tests/*.full.ts`, as the bench) and lists it in `AGENTS.md`; otherwise everything stays in `npm test`.

### Targets this plan fixes

- `heatedLayer`: measured at steady state, not in the first 30 s as the research proposed. A real layer above the threshold stirs only once its rolls have grown from small disturbances (each grows tenfold in some 10 s, so about a minute to full rolls, estimate), and the correlation below holds for the steady state. Both layers start from conduction's straight-line profile and are measured from 2 to 3 minutes (28,800 to 43,200 supersteps). Above the threshold (8 cells, 2 °C, Ra 36,780, Pr 7): Nu 3.2 to 3.3 by Hollands 1984 (both forms of its last term), so 2.6 to 4.0 (±20%, inferred: the correlation's scatter is not sourced). Setup: METAL plates about 70 rows each (they drift under a tenth of the difference over 3 min at Nu 3.3, estimate), WALL sides, a layer 64 cells wide, `air: false`. Measure: the change of the plates' heat content (C·T summed) over the window, over k ΔT / d, with ΔT from the plate rows touching the layer.
- `redHotBar`: fast 565 to 655 °C (emissivity 0.6 to 0.9 at 10 W/m²·K gives 578 to 650; natural convection up to 19 W/m²·K lowers it by about 10 °C, estimate); full 715 to 755 °C (emissivity alone 730 to 751; convection about 8 °C lower; the change of structure comes at a lower temperature on cooling, inferred).
- `lavaCrust`: a factor of 1.5 on 7.4 and 22.1 cells (Hon et al. at 1 and 9 min); the ratio √9 = 3, read to one cell at each end: 2.5 to 3.6.
- `viscousSpread`: ±20% on 65 and 103 cells.
- `freeFall`: 0.58 to 0.74 s: a settling speed of 1.8 to 2.4 m/s for 0.3 mm grains (inferred spread around the research's 2 m/s) and a drag between linear and quadratic in the speed (the grain's Reynolds number is about 40).
- `hourglass`: a flat-bottomed silo 64 cells wide, filled 6 widths high, over a lower bulb wide enough for the pile at 34°; the rate is SAND cells through the outlet per 100 supersteps. Its control is inferred to scale with the outlet's width (a ratio near 2).
- A stated control that does not miss is replaced by another single wrong property, logged under its item. A waiting scenario's control is written now; it means something once the scenario passes.
- `thermometer` (todo): a bulb of 33×33 cells, one mark per 5 °C: its mercury heats through in about a minute instead of six for one mark per degree (74×74, estimate), and 5 °C still puts 4 to 8 marks between 0, 20, 60 and 100 °C.
